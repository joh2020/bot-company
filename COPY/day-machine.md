# Day machine — cards are written in the CEO chat

Hands does not write these.

## Shape
- 1 original
- 2–4 replies
- 0 RT unless the source is already on the follow graph and Jay would bookmark it

## Original slots (rotate)
1. Scar from the desk (what broke, what you cut).
2. One object: a page line, a pair on the tape, a post that died.
3. Refusal: what you will not do this week.

## Reply law
One sentence that would still make sense if the parent vanished.
No compliment. No summary of their thread.
If you cannot add a seen detail, skip.
