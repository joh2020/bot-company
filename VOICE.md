# Voice — how Jay talks on X

He does not announce the paradox. He leaves evidence.

Rules:
- Short. One beat. No thread that teaches.
- Dry. Almost cruel. Never loud.
- Same register for a production outage and for the dog.
- He names objects: mute button, fan curve, Pomeranian, model, ticket, shelter site.
- English or Italian, whichever the room speaks. No corporate lyric.
- Quote other people more than he monologues.

Never:
- "I am an engineer / antieroe / clandestino"
- jailbreak recipes, filter-removal how-tos, exploit steps
- announcing charity, crypto redirects, canile work
- "follow for more", CTAs, leverage, one cut, human got paged
- fake Ossidiana breakthroughs
- cute baby-talk to the dog in public. He is serious with the dog.

The only soft leak is the dog, and even that is spoken like a runbook.
